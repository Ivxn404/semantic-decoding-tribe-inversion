# deck/

Review II (40 % milestone), Review III (60–70 % milestone) and the **final review** presentation for BECE497J Project-I, VIT.

`Final_Sarkar_Choudhuri.pptx` (73 slides) covers the whole project: motivation, literature and novelty, TRIBE v2 and its data, why fMRI, the method, every synthetic result including the barrier test re-verified on 19 Oct 2026, the regional analysis, real fMRI, the recorded multi-start run, standards and ethics, deliverables, live demo, schedule, cost, contributions, PO / PSO / WP mapping, impact, limitations, future work, backup questions and references. Source: `final.js`. Review II and III are kept as presented; their barrier numbers predate the correction.
The `.pptx` files are final. The `.js` files are their pptxgenjs source; rebuilding them needs Node with `pptxgenjs`, `react-icons`, `react-dom` and `sharp`, plus a theme helper (`apply_theme.js`) that is not part of this repository.
